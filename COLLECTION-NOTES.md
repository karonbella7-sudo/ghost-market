# Collection and ending revision

- Every loose street pickup, in both open and closed markets, now uses FOUND feedback. Loose text no longer awards an unrelated random stroke.
- ACHIEVEMENTS is available from the ticket, street, stall and FOUND screens. It lists historical unique assets with origin labels (FOUND, VENDOR, TRADE, HIDDEN MARKET, LANGUAGE), an overall unique-asset progress bar and the stroke completion count. Duplicate assets do not inflate progress; spending does not erase history.
- The total is the union of existing text, stroke and all three vendors' product catalogs. This overall progress is not a new ending gate.
- The ending still becomes available after discovering every stroke type, and starts only upon leaving the street. All acquired text and all historical stroke types enter the composition, including spent strokes.
- FINISH preserves the submitted arrangement, applies a single shared centering transform, draws three connected red curves through every piece and reveals the actual existing Gaussian street canvas behind it. Reduced-motion mode shows stationary lines.
- EXPLORE AGAIN reloads the local demo, resetting visits, collections and the first-two-auspicious rule.
- Open index.html locally; no new network dependencies. Logic is covered by verify-final.cjs; visual smoothness should be checked in the local browser.
